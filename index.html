BETA

What being in beta actually means

SinkOS V2 Public Beta 1 is a real, working build — not a demo. Your account, files, and conversations are stored for real. But it's still under active development, so you may run into rough edges, missing polish, or the occasional bug.

The full version of SinkOS V2 is targeted for October 5. Between now and then, we're using this beta to catch issues before that release — and we're sorry in advance for any interruptions or hiccups along the way.
